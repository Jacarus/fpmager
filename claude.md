##Overview##
First person magic game
Multiplayer pvpve, teams of up to 3

A lot of the mechanics are works in progress, so we will update the design as we go.

##Magic Creation##
Players should design spells outside of the game in a menu
Each spell (depending on their level) has a certain amount of credits to use to create it.
